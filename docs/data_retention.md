# Data retention and deletion

You cannot delete customer data immediately on request: deletion from active systems takes up to 7 days after approval, and backup copies expire within 30 days.
To request deletion of customer data, a workspace administrator must open Settings > Privacy > Delete data and submit the customer identifier.
The deletion request is reviewed within 2 business days; the administrator receives confirmation when active-system deletion completes.

Customer activity records are retained for 90 days unless an approved deletion request removes them sooner. Workspace administrators can export these records before requesting deletion.
